Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most. Question not here? Email us.

When can I download Miga?

This Friday, 27 November, at 14:00 Spanish time (13:00 UTC) on Google Play, free and for everyone.

Is Miga free?

Yes. It's free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. Everything works offline and without signing up. The internet is only used for what you choose: the assistant with your key, Open Food Facts, the packs catalogue and your server.

How much does the cooking assistant cost?

It depends on the provider you pick. OpenRouter has free models; Groq has a free tier and a model on your own computer (Ollama, LM Studio) costs nothing; with Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek or Mistral you pay them for what you use. Miga charges nothing and never sees your data.

Where are my recipes stored?

In your phone's internal storage, and on your own server if you use sync. Miga has no servers of its own.

Can I move my recipes to another phone?

Yes: with a full backup (optionally encrypted), by exporting books as Miga files, or with sync.

Does it help with allergies, coeliac disease or diabetes?

Miga works out allergens and carbohydrate portions from the ingredients to help you choose and plan, but it is a guide: it cannot see brands or labels. Always check each product's label and follow your care team's advice.

Can I save recipes from websites?

Yes. Paste the link and Miga reads the recipe with its photo, times and servings, no assistant needed: almost every recipe site uses a standard format. With the assistant set up, it also polishes it.

And recipe websites?

Those too. In Favourites, paste a cooking website or one of its sections (its recipes page, for example) and you will see its latest recipes with photos. On many websites you can go back to older recipes and search for a particular one. They are imported in one tap, without the assistant when the website uses the standard recipe format.

Can I follow my favourite cooks on YouTube and Instagram?

Yes. In Favourites, paste the link to a YouTube channel or an Instagram profile (or share it to Miga) and you will see their latest videos and reels: 15 on YouTube and 6 on Instagram. Save the ones you like and import them as recipes. Miga reads them directly, without an account; private accounts cannot be followed.

What about recipes from YouTube?

Yes. Paste the link to a video or a Short, or share it from YouTube to Miga. If the description has the recipe or links to the author's website, Miga imports it from there; otherwise Gemini can watch the video (what is said and what is written on screen). The other assistants only read the description.

What about recipes from Instagram?

Yes. Paste the link to a post or reel, or share it from Instagram to Miga. If the caption has the ingredients and steps, Miga reads them without the assistant; otherwise the assistant reads the caption or listens to the video (with Gemini, OpenAI, Groq or Mistral). Private posts cannot be imported.

Is there an iPhone version?

For now Miga is Android only (8.0 or newer).

What does it send to Open Food Facts?

When you scan a barcode or search for a product, that code or text goes to Open Food Facts to fetch its sheet. The data is open (ODbL licence).
